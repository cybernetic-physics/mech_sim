# Static bracket fit

Design a single-piece bracket with two fastener holes (diameter 5.075 mm, hole pitch 36.949 mm).

The bracket must:
* declare `params.declared_min_wall_mm` ≥ 2.646 mm.
* expose ports `mount_a` and `mount_b` attached to the
  fixed bracket body so they are grounded.
* have mobility 0 (everything fixed to ground).
