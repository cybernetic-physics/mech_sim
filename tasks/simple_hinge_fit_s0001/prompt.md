# Simple hinge fit

Design a planar hinge (leaf length 66.66 mm, knuckle Ø4.91 mm).

* Two leaves connected by a revolute joint.
* One leaf is grounded; the other rotates about the
  hinge axis.
* Mobility = 1 (planar revolute).
* Declared pin clearance ≥ 0.1 mm.
* Required ports: `mount_a` (frame on grounded leaf), `hinge_joint` (revolute joint port), `tip_b` (frame on moving leaf).
